01 / Embodied AI Featured

BrushOS

Not text-to-image.
Text-to-paper.

A Gemini 3 agent with a camera, a robot arm, and a real brush. It plans strokes, paints on paper, looks at the result, and decides what to change.

Python / Gemini 3 / Niryo

Watch the demo ↗Explore the code ↗
A closer look

The experiment is the feedback loop, not just the picture. Camera captures and tool-call logs make the agent's decisions inspectable. Large, continuous strokes work around the messy reality of a physical brush. A prototype, with evidence and limitations documented in the repository.

Jatin.Also answers to Legedith.

Technical lead in Hyderabad.
Curious builder, everywhere else.

I work across robotics, AI, and augmented reality. These days, the question pulling me in is how machines behave: what they learn from feedback, how they adapt, and where they fail.

The through-line isn't a language or a framework. It's getting an idea out of my head, into the world, and finding out where it breaks.
